💡 TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS:

✅ DO:
  • Good lighting (not too bright/dark)
  • Clear, sharp image
  • 30-50cm distance
  • Perpendicular to plate
  • Clean plate (no dirt/water)
  • Enable enhancement toggle

❌ DON'T:
  • Blurry or too dark image
  • Angle > 45°
  • Too close (<20cm) or too far (>100cm)
  • Backlit (light behind)
  • Obscured plate
